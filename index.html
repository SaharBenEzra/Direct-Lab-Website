About us

From insurtech hub to the group's innovation engine.

Direct Lab was founded by IDI Direct Insurance in collaboration with MSI, the major Japanese insurer, to identify and promote startups, technologies and entrepreneurs for the digital, direct insurance world.

Our model is an accelerated proof-of-concept.: We aren't an accelerator or mentors — we are your enterprise customer. DirectLab allows startups to test and validate their solutions within live operational environments, working toghther with real customers and key industry players.

Today the hub operates as an innovation service for all Zur Shamir Group companies, insurance, financing and real estate, so one application opens doors across the entire group.

Who we work for

  • IDI Direct Insurance · digital & direct insurance
  • Mimun Yashir · direct financing & credit
  • Adgar · real estate & smart buildings
  • MSI Japan · global reach, Japan & East Asia
